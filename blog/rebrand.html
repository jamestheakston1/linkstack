Beyond the Stack: Why we are becoming LinkLayer.

Today marks the beginning of a new chapter. We’re evolving our identity to clarify our presence and better reflect the infrastructure we’re building for creators.

LinkStack LinkLayer

Coming April 15, 2026

When we launched LinkStack, our goal was simple: to help you organize your links in a single, beautiful "stack." However, as we’ve grown, it has become important for us to forge a unique path. Due to the existence of another platform also named LinkStack, we have decided to transition to a brand that is entirely our own.

Evolving the Identity

This rebranding isn't just about avoiding confusion; it's about our future. A "stack" implies something static—a pile of items. A "layer" represents depth, foundation, and integration. LinkLayer represents our commitment to being the underlying infrastructure of your digital life.

Important Dates & Details

  • Official Launch: The transition will be complete on April 15, 2026.
  • New Domain: Our new home will be linklayer.pages.dev.
  • Automatic Redirects: All your current link-stack.pages.dev links will automatically redirect to the new domain.

The Future is Layered

In the coming months, you'll see a refreshed interface that focuses on speed, accessibility, and modular design. We are introducing new "Layers" for e-commerce, real-time engagement, and verified credentials.

Thank you for being part of this journey. We can't wait to see what you build with LinkLayer.

James Theakston

Founder & CEO, LinkStack
