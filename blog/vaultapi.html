End of Life (EOL)

Vault Protocol V1 has been discontinued. After internal review, it was determined that the protocol's functionality was a 1:1 mirror of our existing Public API.

Legacy Status Updated: Feb 7, 2026

Protocol Redundancy.

We are streamlining the ecosystem. Vault V1 is retiring today to make room for a unified LinkLayer interface.

Efficiency is a core pillar of the LinkStack developer experience. During the final audit of our upcoming programmatic features, we realized that the Vault Protocol V1—specifically our proposed GET/POST handlers—offered no unique utility over our established Public API.

Why EOL?

Rather than maintaining two separate but identical pipelines for data export and link retrieval, we have decided to sunset the Vault-specific endpoints. This ensures that developers have a single, robust point of entry for user data without the confusion of redundant protocols.

Redirecting the Roadmap

  • Public API Remains the primary method for fetching user links and analytics. No changes required for existing integrations.
  • LinkLayer Auth All future authorization and cross-site features are now consolidated into the LinkLayer rebrand launch.

Manual Vault backups (the .json exports) remain fully functional in your dashboard for personal data sovereignty. However, the programmatic "Vault API" mentioned in previous communications will not be proceeding as a standalone service.
