How to get Verified on LinkStack

James Theakston

Founder & Developer • Updated Jan 2026

Verification on LinkStack is more than just a badge—it's a symbol of authenticity and trust. Our verification system ensures that your audience knows they are interacting with the real you, while also unlocking exclusive features on your dashboard.

Why get verified?

Verified profiles receive priority in our internal search algorithm, gain access to advanced custom branding options, and display the official blue (or gold) checkmark next to their name. This helps prevent impersonation and establishes your professional presence.

The Checklist

Business Pro Status (Free)

Enable Business Pro in your dashboard for $0. This unlocks the analytics and tools required for a verified presence.

Complete Profile

Your profile must have a valid avatar image and a bio longer than 3 characters that accurately describes who you are.

Active Links

You need at least 2 active links in your stack. This demonstrates that your page is functional and ready for your audience.

How to Apply

Once you have met all the criteria listed above, head over to the Verification tab in your dashboard. If the checklist is complete, a "Claim Badge" button will appear. Simply click it, and your profile will be instantly updated with your new badge.

Ready to start?

Head back to your dashboard to see your progress and unlock your badge today.
